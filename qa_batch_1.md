## Q1: [New Company Law] For Expatriates: How to protect trademark rights against infringement?

**A**: Trademark infringement remedies: 1) Collect and preserve evidence including notarized purchases; 2) Send cease and desist letter; 3) File complaint with market supervision authority; 4) File lawsuit for injunction and damages; 5) Compensation based on actual losses, infringer profits, or royalty multiples.

**法律依据**: 《反不正当竞争法》

**领域**: 知识产权 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Securities Crime] For Expatriates: What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《社会保险法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English
